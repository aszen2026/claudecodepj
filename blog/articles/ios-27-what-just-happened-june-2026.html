ios 27: What Just Happened (June 2026)

What Happened

Apple's WWDC 2026 keynote streams this Monday, June 8 at 10 a.m. Pacific — two days out — and the marquee announcement is iOS 27. After more than a year of delays, this is the release where Apple's long-promised "more personal" Siri is expected to finally ship as a genuine conversational assistant, rebuilt on a Google Gemini model rather than Apple's own.

The key facts reported ahead of the keynote:

  • The engine swap: On January 12, 2026, Apple and Google confirmed a multi-year deal — estimated by Bloomberg's Mark Gurman at roughly $1 billion per year — giving Apple a custom 1.2-trillion-parameter Gemini model. That's about eight times larger than Apple's existing ~150-billion-parameter cloud model, and it uses a mixture-of-experts design.
  • Privacy framing: The Gemini weights run inside Apple's Private Cloud Compute — on Apple silicon, not Google's servers — and Apple says no user data is shared with Google.
  • Already live: The first Gemini-powered Siri features arrived quietly in iOS 26.4 this spring; iOS 27 is set to bring the full redesign.
  • Rollout: Developer beta on June 8, public beta in early July, general release in September 2026.
  • Compatibility: iOS 27 is expected to support iPhone 12 and newer, dropping the iPhone 11 line and second-generation iPhone SE.

Why It Matters

For an industry that spent 2024 and 2025 asking whether Apple could build a frontier model, iOS 27 answers bluntly: it won't try alone. Renting Gemini to run the core of Siri is the most consequential AI concession Apple has ever made — and it reshuffles the field.

  • Google wins biggest. Gemini gets distribution to an installed base north of 1.4 billion iPhones, plus a recurring ~$1B check and powerful validation of its model-as-infrastructure business.
  • OpenAI loses ground. ChatGPT was the original Apple Intelligence partner in iOS 18. With Gemini now powering Siri's brain, ChatGPT slips from default to optional add-on.
  • Users finally get a real assistant. The reported feature set — on-screen awareness, personal context, cross-app actions, and a dedicated Siri app with chat history and a text mode — is the Siri Apple demoed in 2024 but never shipped.
  • Developers get a new surface. Siri's ability to act inside and between apps runs on App Intents, making that framework the price of entry for agentic features.
💡
For developers: If your app doesn't expose App Intents yet, that gap now separates apps Siri can operate from apps it can't. Prioritize it before the September release.

Context

iOS 27 lands one year after WWDC 2025, where Apple introduced both year-based version names (iOS 26, macOS 26) and the "Liquid Glass" interface overhaul. By contrast, iOS 27 is reported to be a stability-and-battery release in the mold of Snow Leopard — the visible UI changes are modest, and the substance is the AI plumbing underneath.

The Gemini deal also fits a clear pattern: platform owners increasingly partner for frontier intelligence rather than own it. Samsung already ships Gemini in Galaxy AI; Microsoft built Copilot on OpenAI. Apple, the last big holdout, has now joined them. Beyond Siri, the reported iOS 27 list includes:

  • Photos: AI editing tools called Extend, Enhance, and Reframe, the last changing perspective in 3D spatial photos.
  • Wallet: creating digital passes from physical cards and tickets, plus bill-splitting from a photographed receipt.
  • Safari: automated tab grouping.
  • Connectivity: 5G satellite support, likely limited to the coming iPhone 18 Pro models.

What To Do Next

Nothing in iOS 27 is final until the keynote — treat the June 8 stream (10 a.m. Pacific) as the moment the rumors become commitments. Until then:

  • Users: Skip the June 8 developer beta. Wait for the July public beta, install it on a secondary device, and back up to iCloud or a Mac first.
  • iPhone 11 / SE (2nd-gen) owners: Assume your device drops off the support list. Budget for an upgrade to at least an iPhone 12 if you want the new Siri.
  • Developers: Adopt App Intents now, and book the post-keynote sessions on Siri, App Intents, and Private Cloud Compute. Audit what user data your Siri features touch before the public beta.
  • Buyers: If satellite 5G matters to you, hold off — that feature is tied to this fall's iPhone 18 Pro hardware, not iOS 27 on existing phones.
💡
Watch the right signal: Apple shipped a partial Siri in iOS 26.4 and over-promised in 2024. Judge the keynote by whether the on-screen-awareness and cross-app demos are framed as shipping in September — not "coming later this year."
